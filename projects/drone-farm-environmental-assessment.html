Drone Survey and Environmental Assessment of a 4,132 ha FarmCase Study

UAV photogrammetry over a 4,132 ha agricultural estate in the Campos Gerais region of Paraná: orthomosaics, elevation model, 11-class land-use map, Forest Code regularity of APP and legal reserve, and a field-ready erosion inventory.

Geospatial / Drone Survey
2022 - 2023
Campos Gerais, Paraná, Brazil
18,780 aerial photos

Project Overview

A farm group needed two answers about one of its estates: whether its permanent preservation areas (APP) and legal reserve were in order under the Forest Code, and where soil erosion was eating into productive land. Satellite imagery is too coarse for rills and gullies, so the whole property was flown with a drone.

18,780 aerial photos taken over 14 field days were processed into orthomosaics and an elevation model, and every hectare was mapped into 11 land-use classes. APP along watercourses, native vegetation and consolidated areas were delineated and tested against the 22 July 2008 cut-off of the Forest Code; erosion features were digitised by type (sheet, rill and gully) and cross-tabulated with land use.

The estate was confirmed in regularity: 1,511.5 ha of native vegetation, of which 493.2 ha are riparian APP, with only 56.7 ha of consolidated use inside APP, all opened before 2008. The erosion inventory located 19.8 ha of active erosion and showed pasture as the most affected use (3.3% of its area), giving the farm a prioritised list for soil conservation work.

Methodology

  1. Flight planning over the property boundary and 14 days of drone flights (18,780 photos).
  2. Photogrammetric processing into orthomosaics and an elevation model, with slope and contours.
  3. Land-use mapping in 11 classes: agriculture 40.8%, native vegetation 36.4%, planted forest 13.7%, pasture 7.8%, plus roads, buildings, ponds and fallow.
  4. APP delineation along watercourses and comparison of native vegetation and consolidated areas against the 2008 cut-off, cross-checked with the CAR.
  5. Erosion inventory by type (sheet, rill, gully) cross-tabulated with land use, with management recommendations per class.

Deliverables

  • Environmental assessment report with land-use, APP and erosion tables.
  • A3 thematic map atlas (overview, agriculture, pasture, planted forest, native vegetation, erosion).
  • Orthomosaics, elevation model, shapefiles and KML for field teams.
