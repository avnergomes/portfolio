Rural Environmental Registry (CAR) Retification, 4,537 haCase Study

Environmental compliance diagnosis and CAR retification for a 4,537 ha Atlantic Forest property inside a state environmental protection area on the coast of Paraná, reconciled with the land title (SIGEF), the ADA and the rural land tax (ITR).

Geospatial / Environmental Compliance
2026
Coastal Paraná, Brazil
4,537 ha, in progress

Project Overview

The owners needed their CAR, Brazil's federal rural environmental registry, to reflect the property as it really is, with the same numbers carried into the ADA (environmental declaratory act) and the ITR rural land tax return. The registered CAR declared no consolidated area at all, 344 ha of permanent preservation areas (APP) and a proposed legal reserve of 980.6 ha, on a property that lies entirely inside a state environmental protection area.

The diagnosis rebuilt every layer from independent sources: the certified INCRA/SIGEF perimeter, hydrography-based APP, land use, slope from the Copernicus DEM and every CAR that overlaps the property. It found 68 third-party CAR records overlapping the estate, and an APP of 1,029 ha when computed from the FBDS hydrography, three times the declared figure.

Because the Forest Code (Law 12,651/2012) treats land opened before 22 July 2008 as consolidated, the key question was which cleared areas existed on that date. Instead of a field survey over occupied land, the evidence came from the 2008 record itself: a Landsat 7 scene taken six days before the legal cut-off, Landsat 5, NDVI, Hansen loss year, the MapBiomas 1985-2024 series and JAXA PALSAR forest maps, compiled per polygon. Registry deeds back to 1971 were traced to recover the original legal reserve commitments.

97.4 ha in 46 polygons proven open in 2008 (consolidated), 15.5 ha identified as opened after 2008, and the CAR perimeter matched to the certified SIGEF perimeter within 0.76 m. The owners get a defensible retification instead of a registry whose APP differs by 685 ha from the hydrography reference.

Methodology

  1. Reconcile the certified SIGEF perimeter with the declared CAR geometry and lock the reference area.
  2. Rebuild APP from the FBDS hydrography and compare it with the declared layer; map native vegetation, land use and slope (MapBiomas, Dynamic World, Copernicus DEM).
  3. Pull every overlapping CAR from SICAR and document the overlaps for an official cancellation request.
  4. Build the 22 July 2008 evidence per polygon from Landsat 5 and 7, NDVI, Hansen, MapBiomas and JAXA PALSAR.
  5. Encode the area invariants as 9 automated tests that rerun on every revision.
  6. Carry the retified numbers into the ADA and the ITR so all three declarations agree.
