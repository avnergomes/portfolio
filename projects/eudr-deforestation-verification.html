Project Overview

Under the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR), an importer must show that every plot behind a shipment had no deforestation after 31 December 2020. A Belgian importer of South American tropical timber asked for a four-origin pilot to compare against the verification it was already buying: a natural forest concession in Peru, one in Bolivia, a managed forest unit in the Brazilian Amazon and a pine plantation estate in southern Brazil.

Each origin got its own evidence chain. In Brazil the verdict comes from the official INPE records (PRODES and DETER), checked against the federal rural registry (CAR/SICAR). In Peru and Bolivia two independent global datasets, JRC Tropical Moist Forest and Hansen Global Forest Change, were read straight from public cloud-optimized archives, with no Earth Engine dependency, on a stable 1-hectare grid.

The hard part was not finding loss pixels but deciding which loss counts. Selective logging under an approved management plan and the clear-cut harvest of a planted pine stand both appear as tree-cover loss in global data, yet neither is deforestation under the EUDR. The pipeline separates them, so the report flags conversion, not forestry.

On the pine estate, global data alone would have reported 1,405.6 ha of deforestation. The official Atlantic Forest record confirmed 25.34 ha of native vegetation removal after 2020, in 7 polygons; 1,167.9 ha of the global loss sat in plantation cells, which is harvest, not conversion.

Methodology

  1. Validate geometries and CRS (WGS 84 and SIRGAS 2000), match each plot to its CAR receipt and remove duplicated features before any area is counted.
  2. Brazil: PRODES clear-cut and DETER deforestation from the TerraBrasilis WFS decide the verdict, with coverage proven against the biome mask; DETER degradation, Hansen and TMF stay as context.
  3. Peru and Bolivia: JRC TMF v1_2025 (deforestation and degradation year) and Hansen GFC v1.13 read by window from public buckets, cut-off after 2020, summarised on a 1 ha grid anchored to the CRS origin.
  4. Plantation rule: the MapBiomas silviculture class for 2020 is joined to Hansen loss per cell, so harvest and replanting of planted forest is classified as clean.
  5. Every flagged area checked against dated Sentinel-2 imagery; a revision round moved the Peru and Bolivia flags to review after the imagery showed no conversion.
  6. One three-page PDF per origin (verdict per plot, satellite evidence panels, methodology), cross-checked against the JSON outputs before delivery.

Technologies

  • Reproducible Python pipeline that reruns on every PRODES, DETER and JRC update.
